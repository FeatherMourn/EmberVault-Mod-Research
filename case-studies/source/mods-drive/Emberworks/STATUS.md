## Status meanings

- `offline_foundation`: package behavior exists and is tested without the game.
- `offline_simulator`: package simulates the intended system and is tested.
- `probe_required`: game/runtime behavior still needs direct evidence.
- `runtime_verified`: behavior confirmed in Enshrouded with readback and persistence evidence.

The project is not 100% complete until runtime capabilities are tested and the
completion audit is updated with direct evidence.
