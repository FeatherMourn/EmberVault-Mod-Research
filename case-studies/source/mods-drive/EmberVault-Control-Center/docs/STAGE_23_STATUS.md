# Stage 23 — Character Tools and Trainer Expansion

Stage 23 expands planning and guarded Trainer workflows without granting either
feature authority to mutate saves or live game state.

## Delivered

- Character build-template metadata and versioned plan updates.
- Plan-only progression simulation and equipment comparison outputs.
- Save-linked planning through existing checksum-valid backup gates.
- Trainer test steps, recovery simulation state, and evidence-gate status.
- Control Center summaries and actions for character planning and Trainer review.

## Safety boundary

Character exports remain `plan-only`. Trainer plans require the isolated research
profile and a verified backup; recovery simulation is evidence preparation, not
save restoration or gameplay mutation. Separate-process launch and capability
governance remain authoritative for any future Trainer worker.
