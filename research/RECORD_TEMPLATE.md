# Research record template

Copy this template for a durable investigation. Keep raw captures and generated output in the relevant experiment area; this record should explain what they establish.

## Metadata

- ID:
- Title:
- Topic:
- Status: `confirmed` / `strongly-supported` / `working-hypothesis` / `disproved` / `obsolete`
- Confidence: `high` / `medium` / `low`
- Game build/version:
- Research date:
- Related project:

## Question

What are we trying to determine?

## Method

Describe the static/runtime/data-probing method, required tools, setup, and safety constraints.

## Evidence

Link source files, captures, tables, logs, addresses, symbols, or reproduction scripts. Record what was directly observed.

## Result

State the smallest conclusion supported by the evidence.

## Interpretation

Separate inference from observation. Explain alternative interpretations and remaining uncertainty.

## Modding implications

Explain what this enables, what it does not enable, and what implementation boundary or risk remains.

## Reproduction

List the exact steps needed to repeat the result on the recorded game build.

## Follow-up

List the next experiment that would increase confidence or resolve the main uncertainty.
