# Research Lab guide

Research Lab is the controlled path between a content project and a normal game session.

1. Open **Research Lab** and select a validated project.
2. Click **Start guided test** to prepare a temporary profile with third-party mods isolated.
3. Launch the controlled session and follow the in-game checklist one observation at a time.
4. Capture a screenshot or record the result, then catalog what was actually observed.
5. Finish the session and restore the normal profile before returning to ordinary play.

Control Center labels results as proven, experimental, or still unverified. A package passing validation does not by itself prove runtime registration, visual rendering, or persistence.
