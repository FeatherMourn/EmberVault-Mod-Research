# Reading Embervault status labels

## Verified route

The documented route has passed the evidence required for the specific boundary described. It does not automatically mean every related feature is verified.

## Experimental

The route is usable for controlled testing, but important behavior, compatibility, or safety questions remain.

## Research-only

The work belongs in an isolated test profile or disposable world. It should not be treated as a stable installation.

## Blocked

The current evidence identifies a missing dependency, unsafe boundary, or unresolved failure that prevents promotion.
