# Capability audit baseline

- Status: reference baseline
- Recorded build: 1076226
- Source: `research/CAPABILITY_AUDIT_20260927.json` in EnshroudedModHub

The audit separates verified, experimental, research-only, and unsupported capabilities. Embervault should preserve that distinction in every public record rather than treating successful offline generation as proof of in-game behavior.

## Publishing rule

A capability should move toward stable status only after its runtime boundary, current-build compatibility, and relevant in-game behavior are independently documented.
